Following the 2026 midterm elections, Americans came together in an unprecedented grassroots effort to demand leadership that reflected the interests of the voters who brought the Democratic coalition into power. Across the country, we organized through social media, signed petitions, contacted members of Congress, and made our voices heard.

The party establishment chose to ignore us.

If our elected officials prioritize their donors over the people they represent, then we will take our message directly to those donors. If money is what speaks loudest in Washington, we will make our voices heard where Washington is listening.

The People Over Power Coalition was established for that purpose. Our immediate focus is the Democratic Party establishment's decision to nominate Hakeem Jeffries for Speaker of the House despite widespread grassroots opposition.
