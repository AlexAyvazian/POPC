Business & Organization Identity Research

This research directory is part of the combined eight-committee analysis. By default it prioritizes actual PAC / organization receipt identities, researched identities, employers reported by two or more distinct individual donors, and single-donor employers whose donor reports a high-influence executive role such as CEO/C-suite, president, founder/owner, partner/principal, board leadership, vice president, or executive/managing director. All other employer names remain preserved and searchable.

Open a name to see original reported forms, individual donors, receiving committees, identity scope, parent-company context and source links. Memo records remain visible for identity research, but memo amounts are excluded from contribution totals.
