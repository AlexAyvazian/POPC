Sources and how totals are counted

Totals sum non-memo itemized receipts in the uploaded files. Memo and employer amounts are not added again. Transfers between linked committees may be counted in more than one committee; this is not a deduplicated measure of external fundraising or the full FEC cycle total.

Source: the eight existing POPC committee datasets, derived from supplied Federal Election Commission exports. Jeffries Clark Aguilar Victory Fund is represented by the one supplied receipt. The underlying committee pages retain filing links and memo records. Normalized reported names consolidate exact matches only; name variants and parent-company affiliations require separate verification.
